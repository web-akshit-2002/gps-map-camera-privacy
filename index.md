# Privacy Policy

**GPS Map Camera: Time & Place** (`com.timeplace.gpsmapcamera`)

**Privacy contact** [Contact the developer on GitHub](https://github.com/web-akshit-2002/gps-map-camera-privacy/issues/new). Please keep personal information, photos and GPS coordinates out of public issues.

## Photos, location and storage

The app uses your camera to take photos and uses location permission to add a map, address, coordinates, and other location details to your photo stamps. The app requests foreground location permissions. Your captures, capture records, settings and map caches are stored on your device. Photos you share can include location in the visible stamp and photo metadata. Recipients can read that information.

## Online services

- Map requests use OpenFreeMap or Esri ArcGIS, depending on the selected map style. The providers receive your IP address and tile requests that identify the requested map area.
- Address lookup uses the Android geocoder. Depending on your device and provider, the coordinates may be processed online to return an address.
- When you use weather information, approximate coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to retrieve local weather.
- The free edition uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for banners, interstitials and optional rewarded ads. Google may process information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostics to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Where available, use the app's privacy options to manage advertising choices. See [Google's advertising privacy information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).
- Google Play Billing processes optional Remove Ads purchases. The app uses purchase status to unlock and restore the entitlement. Payment details are handled by Google Play.

If you send a support report, the recipient receives what you choose to send, including any diagnostic information you choose to attach.

## Retention, security and deletion

Saved photos remain on your device until you delete them. You can remove photos from your gallery, remove downloaded areas in Offline maps, and clear app data through Android Settings. Uninstalling removes the app's private data. Photos saved to your device's shared gallery may remain after uninstalling. The app keeps its local photo verification key in Android Keystore. Third-party services handle and retain the information they receive under their own policies.
